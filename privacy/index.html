Merswel OS Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026.

Operator and data controller: Merswel LLC
Contact: rjwelbeck@merswel.com

1. Scope and current availability

This policy describes Merswel OS's private-preview application and the planned Google Workspace connection. Google Workspace is not currently connected. Descriptions of Google access below apply when that connection is enabled and you authorize it; they do not imply that all planned functions are already available. The public Merswel website is an informational trust surface hosted on GitHub Pages, with no contact form.

2. Account, organization, and business information

Merswel OS uses account identity and confirmation information, organization names and identifiers, membership roles, and organization-scoped access records to authenticate users and determine access. Authorized organization users can record business information such as clients, contacts, requests, engagements, and delivery work. These records are processed to provide the organization's workspace and its requested functions.

3. Connected-provider data

Connecting a provider requires an authorized organization user to approve the requested permissions. When you connect Google Workspace, the intended initial functionality may access Google account identity, granted permissions, and connection-health metadata; Gmail messages, threads, and attachment metadata for requested reading/search and draft preparation or explicitly requested sending; Drive metadata and files specifically authorized for the app; Calendar events and availability for read-first scheduling assistance; and Contacts for requested lookup/matching.
Initial Google work does not include calendar writes, contact writes/automatic imports or merges, or unrestricted bulk indexing of Drive content. No access to these services starts merely because you use the Merswel homepage.

4. Purpose, Limited Use, and restrictions

Provider data is used only to provide user-requested Merswel functionality, such as communications, file search/access, scheduling assistance, and contact lookup. Merswel does not sell provider data or use it for advertising.
Merswel OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: Google API Services User Data Policy
Google data will not be transferred to AI models or used for model training, and human access will not occur outside the access permitted by that policy. Planned provider actions require their own explicit authorization and qualification; this policy is not authorization to activate a provider.

5. Credential custody and security

The implemented connection foundation encrypts provider authorization credentials in protected server-side custody and binds credential handling to an organization and connection. Browser-visible connection information is separate from credential material. Server-side checks verify organizational membership and the permitted role before connection operations. These protections do not guarantee security and are not a claim of security certification.

6. Service providers and sharing

Merswel OS uses Supabase for authentication and database services and ChatGPT Sites for application hosting. The public Merswel informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include a contact form or embedded reCAPTCHA. When a Google connection is enabled, requests necessary to perform authorized operations are sent to Google.
This policy does not represent that an AI provider currently receives Google data. Any new provider or additional data flow must be reviewed and disclosed before activation. Processing regions, contractual details, and any required jurisdiction-specific notices remain matters for the operator's review; this text does not invent them.

7. Retention

The implemented connection foundation stores connection identity, scopes, lifecycle metadata, encrypted credentials, and audit events. It does not implement a fixed automatic deletion schedule for connection metadata or audit history. Organization business records are not deleted by disconnecting a provider. No specific retention or backup-deletion period is promised by this policy. Retention of future fetched Google content and its operational logging must be determined before live Google data processing begins.

8. Disconnect and revocation

The implemented local disconnect lifecycle removes stored provider credentials and records the disconnected state, while retaining connection metadata and audit history. Provider-side revocation is a separate operation and is confirmed only when the provider confirms it. The live Google connection and hosted controls are not yet available. You may also review and revoke permissions through your Google account. Local disconnection is not proof that Google has revoked a grant, and does not delete content in Google.

9. Privacy and deletion requests

Email rjwelbeck@merswel.com to request information about your account or organization data or to request deletion. Merswel may need to verify your identity and authority for the relevant organization before handling a request. Self-service account deletion is not currently provided. The operator will review applicable retention requirements, audit records, and backup limitations when responding; no automatic deletion deadline or immediate backup purge is promised.

10. Changes and contact

Merswel LLC will review this policy before new data practices or provider capabilities are activated. Contact rjwelbeck@merswel.com with product or privacy questions. Applicable legal rights are not limited by this policy. This text is product disclosure, not legal advice or a certification of compliance; Founder/legal review remains appropriate before wider external use.
